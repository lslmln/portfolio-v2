## The brief

- **Positioning:** Design engineer — someone who designs *and* builds. Functional, polished, and good to use. Someone you can trust to deliver both halves.
- **Tone:** Sharp and professional over playful. Fintech-appropriate formality — but with a twist, not stiff-corporate.
- **Motifs to explore:** AI elements and/or general engineering elements, worked in — not necessarily literal iconography.
- **Interaction:** Lots of micro-interactions. The whole thing should feel seamless, not decorated with motion.
- Explicitly: find your own aesthetic character — don't copy any reference wholesale. References are each here for one quality, not to be recreated.

## Emerging patterns (synthesis from conversation, not just raw pins)

- **Organic mark against structure.** Ink splotch (bleibtgleich), grain texture (thetiebreak), hand-drawn sketches (carlhauser) — three independent pins where an imperfect, human mark sits against an otherwise structured/technical system. This may be closer to "the twist" than any literal AI/engineering icon: precision as the base, a visible human hand breaking it.
- **Discoverable, unannounced delight.** huyml's cuboid home button isn't labeled or presented as a feature — it rewards curiosity instead of demanding attention. This is a way to reconcile "sharp/professional" with "a twist": the personality doesn't have to be visible by default, it can live entirely in earned moments.
- **Abstract over literal motif.** thetiebreak's tennis-court outline is a domain motif (the sport) worked into the layout as pure line/structure, not an illustration or icon. Direct answer to "how literal should the AI/engineering motifs be" — this is the abstract end of that spectrum, working.
- **PCB/circuit photography → reframe as schematic, not photo.** A literal photo of a circuit board is hard to use directly. The underlying logic (traces connecting nodes, routed paths) might work better as an actual *diagram/schematic* device (e.g. a nav or process-flow rendered as a routed path) than as imagery to place on the page.

## Reference pins (21, grouped by theme)

### Nav / scroll micro-interactions
- **[siberia.es](https://siberia.es/)** — nav text color adapts against whatever background scrolls under it, stays legible without a fixed bar. `adaptive-nav, contrast`
- **[outfit.hellohello.is](https://outfit.hellohello.is/)** — same adaptive-nav trick, color inverts/shifts against the background. `adaptive-nav, contrast`
- **[butter.video](https://www.butter.video/)** — nav responds to scroll. `nav, scroll-interaction, micro-interaction`
- **[gionatannese.com/about](https://www.gionatannese.com/about)** — kinetic type + cursor-follow: surrounding letters shift left/right while a center portrait floats down and tracks the cursor. `cursor-follow, kinetic-type, micro-interaction`
- **[huyml.co](https://huyml.co/)** — home button is a 3D cuboid with a playful scroll interaction, top-left, unlabeled — found through discovery. `discoverability, playful-detail, 3d, easter-egg`

### Texture / organic mark-making
- **[bleibtgleich.dev](https://bleibtgleich.dev/)** — ink splotch aesthetic, organic mark-making. `ink, texture, unique`
- **[thetiebreak.merci-michel.com](https://thetiebreak.merci-michel.com/eu/en/)** — grainy/noise texture; tennis-court line markings worked into the layout as an outline, not decoration. `grain, texture, subtle-motif`

### Sketch / blueprint
- **[carlhauser.com](https://www.carlhauser.com/)** — white background, hand-drawn sketches of physical items held in a grid of boxes. The reference for the blueprint/sketch quality. `sketch, grid, white-space, hand-drawn`

## Candidate aesthetic directions (discussed, not yet re-attempted)

Five directions were named and discussed conversationally, then sketched as a first visual pass (see Tooling note below) — the user's verdict on that pass was **"generic"**, which is why the reference-gathering above happened before trying again:

- **A — Instrument panel:** cockpit/mixing-console feel, graphite/gunmetal base, tabular monospace, modular panels, one precise accent reserved for "live" states, snappy/mechanical micro-interactions.
- **B — Field notes:** notebook/journal framing crossed with design-engineer identity, annotations, process marks, AI motif as margin notes rather than a chatbot.
- **C — Guided terminal:** leans into cloudstudio.es literally — the site narrates/reveals itself as you move, terminal conventions used sparingly.
- **D — The ledger:** fintech's real visual vocabulary (statements, line items, a running balance, a verified stamp) reframed as portfolio structure, not blue/checkmark clichés.
- **E — Build log:** career milestones as changelog entries, a project card that flips to show a before/after "diff" on hover, a quiet craft-quality "passing" badge.

## Open questions (still unresolved)

- **Literalness:** How literal should the AI/engineering motifs be — actual iconography vs. abstract structural cues? (thetiebreak's court outline is a working example of the abstract end.)
- **Restraint vs. twist:** Where does "sharp and professional" sit relative to "a twist"? (huyml's unannounced discoverability is one possible answer — the twist lives in earned moments, not the default view.)
- **Theme:** Light-first, dark-first, or genuinely both?
- **Range:** One committed idea vs. a more composed, layered system?
